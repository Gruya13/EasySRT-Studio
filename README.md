## Why Creators Choose EasySRT Studio

- **Zero Setup Hassle**: The Windows installer sets everything up automatically in one step — just launch the app, choose a free offline translator or plug in a Cloud API key, and start captioning.
- **Total Offline Privacy or Turbo Speed**: Run **100% locally** on your GPU/CPU with zero cloud uploads, or pair local GPU transcription with fast cloud translation to process batch projects up to **28x faster than real-time**.
- **Accent-Resilient English & Serbian Auto-Detect**: Automatic language detection focuses accurately on English and Serbian so non-native accents don't drift into unrelated languages, while still giving you manual selection for German, Spanish, French, Italian, Russian, Greek, and more.
- **Broadcast Timing & Studio-Grade Serbian Support**: Clean two-line TV/Film captions or punchy 1-line Shorts/Reels subtitles, 1-click reading-speed tightening (**Auto-Fit CPS**), polished Serbian Ekavian, and instant **Latin ↔ Cyrillic** switching.
- **Precision Waveform Timeline**: Scrub an interactive 3-track audio and subtitle timeline, drag cue edges to fine-tune timing, and zoom smoothly with your mouse wheel up to **2000%**.

## Get Started in 3 Steps

1. **Install**: Download the **Windows Setup `.exe`** or **Linux `.AppImage`** from **[GitHub Releases](https://github.com/Gruya13/EasySRT-Studio/releases/latest)**.
2. **Choose Your Translator**: Pick a free local offline AI model tailored to your graphics card, or connect **DeepSeek / OpenAI-compatible** APIs.
3. **Drop Media & Export**: Drag in `.mp3`, `.mp4`, `.wav`, `.m4a`, or existing `.srt`/`.vtt` subtitles, refine on the timeline, and export studio-ready **`.SRT`**, **`.VTT`**, or **`.TXT`**.
